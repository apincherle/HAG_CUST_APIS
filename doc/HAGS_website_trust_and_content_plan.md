# HAGS Website Trust & Content Plan

## Purpose

The primary objective of the HAGS website should be to answer three questions immediately:

1. What does HAGS grade?
2. Why should collectors trust HAGS?
3. What happens after I submit my item?

Every page should reinforce professionalism, consistency, transparency, security, **accessibility**, and **inclusion**.

HAGS serves **all collectors** — regardless of background, ability, age, gender identity, sexual orientation, ethnicity, faith, or experience level. The website and customer experience should make that commitment visible and practical, not implied.

# Accessibility & Inclusion (Site-Wide Standards)

Apply these requirements to **every page**, component, email template, and PDF linked from the site.

## Accessibility (WCAG-oriented)

Aim for **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** as the baseline (AAA where practical for key flows).

### Content & language

* Use plain language; avoid jargon unless defined in context.
* Structure pages with a single logical **H1** and nested headings (H2, H3) — do not skip levels.
* Write meaningful **link text** (e.g. “Read grading standards” not “Click here”).
* Provide **text alternatives** for all meaningful images, charts, and gallery examples (alt text that describes what matters for grading, not “image1”).
* Caption or provide transcripts for **video** walkthroughs and grader introductions.
* Ensure **colour is not the only** way to convey grade bands or status (use labels, patterns, or icons plus text).

### Design & interaction

* Minimum **contrast ratios**: 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and UI components (verify with a contrast checker).
* All interactive elements must be **keyboard-operable** with a visible **focus indicator**.
* Touch targets at least **44×44 CSS pixels** where possible on mobile.
* Forms: associate every field with a **visible label**; surface errors in text (not colour alone) with clear recovery steps.
* Support **zoom up to 200%** without loss of content or functionality.
* Avoid content that **flashes** in a way that could trigger seizures.
* Respect **`prefers-reduced-motion`** for animations and carousels.

### Technical checklist

* Semantic HTML (`nav`, `main`, `footer`, `button` vs `div` clicks).
* Skip link: “Skip to main content” at the top of each page.
* **Language** attribute on `<html>` (e.g. `lang="en-GB"`).
* Test with **screen readers** (e.g. NVDA, VoiceOver) on submit flow and FAQ.
* Publish an **Accessibility Statement** (footer link): scope, known limitations, contact for barriers, review date.

## Open to all collectors

* Use **inclusive imagery** that reflects diverse collectors, items, and communities — avoid stock that implies only one demographic or niche.
* **No gatekeeping language** (“real collectors only”, “serious men”, etc.).
* Pricing, service levels, and turnaround tables must be **readable and comparable** (accessible tables with headers).
* Offer **multiple ways to contact support** (form, email, phone if available) and state expected response times.
* If the site is English-first, say so clearly and note how non-English speakers can get help.

## LGBTQ+ inclusion & respect

HAGS welcomes **LGBTQ+ collectors, staff, and partners** as full members of the community we serve.

### Content & tone

* Use **gender-neutral** default copy where appropriate: “collectors”, “customers”, “you/your” rather than assuming gender.
* On forms and accounts, allow **optional pronouns** and **title** fields (including Mx, no title, prefer not to say) — never require disclosure of gender or orientation to submit items.
* Avoid stereotypes in examples, team bios, and marketing.
* When celebrating community (events, Pride, partnerships), do so **authentically** — year-round inclusion, not only seasonal logos.

### Policies (publish or link from footer)

* **Equal treatment**: grading and support decisions are based on published standards and service terms — not personal characteristics.
* **Anti-harassment**: zero tolerance for abuse of staff or other customers based on LGBTQ+ identity or any protected characteristic.
* **Privacy**: do not share a customer’s sexual orientation or gender identity without explicit consent.
* **Staff**: foster an inclusive workplace; optional ally/LGBTQ+ network or training for customer-facing roles.

### Suggested on-site copy (short trust line — homepage or footer)

> HAGS is committed to an accessible, welcoming experience for every collector — including LGBTQ+ people and disabled users. If you need a reasonable adjustment or have feedback on inclusion, please contact us.

We take consistency and accuracy seriously. Every item is assessed using our published grading standards and undergoes quality-control checks before certification.

If you have questions regarding a completed grade, our team will be happy to review the grading report and explain the factors that contributed to the final assessment.

In the rare event that a grading or labelling error is identified, we will investigate the matter and take appropriate corrective action.

Our goal is to provide grading decisions that are transparent, consistent, and supported by our published standards.

# Success Criteria

A first-time visitor should be able to answer the following within 60 seconds:

✓ What does HAGS do?

✓ How does grading work?

✓ Why should I trust them?

✓ How long will it take?

✓ What happens if I have a question?

✓ How do I submit my item?

✓ Can I use this site if I have a disability or assistive technology?

✓ Will I be treated with respect regardless of who I am (including LGBTQ+ identity)?

If those questions are answered clearly, conversion rates and customer confidence will increase significantly.
